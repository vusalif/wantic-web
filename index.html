Wantic shows who is on your site, from the Mac menu bar. Live visitors, today, and recent visits. One script tag in the page head.

FAQ

What does it do?

It shows who is on your site, from the menu bar.

Which Macs?

macOS 27 and later.

How does a site get counted?

Paste one script tag into the page head.

What is in the panel?

Live visitors, today, the last 24 hours, countries, and recent visits.

Does it open a window?

No. The number stays next to the menu bar icon.
